    Conversion · FintechProduct case · SPJIMR
    P

    Paytm: fixing the recharge funnel

    Only 35% of users landing on bill pay complete a transaction, and 60% return the next month.

    Bet
    PayExpress: single-tap pay with auto-applied coupons and plan nudges
    Metric
    Transaction completion time and rate
    Full breakdown

    Pain points from the journey map

    • Complex navigation, information overload, limited payment options, a cumbersome checkout

    Proposed features

    • PayExpress (quick win, high impact): single-tap pay, auto-applied best coupon, recommendation nudges
    • Paytm Central (major project): personalized home with priority alerts, consolidated view and voice search
    • AutoBot (fill-in): SMS-based data tracking, auto-pay mandates, multi-account management

    Risks I planned for

    • Auto-applied coupons users dislike, irrelevant recommendations, nudge fatigue, alert overload

    Launch

    Pilot with users near their recharge date who use Paytm moderately, with test and control groups, then refine from heatmaps and drop-off data.
